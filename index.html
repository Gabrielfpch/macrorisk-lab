04 · TRANSPARENCY

Methodology

Every assumption is visible in the source code.

01

Expected risk

Portfolio return is the weighted average of asset assumptions. Volatility uses a full covariance matrix.

02

Tail risk

VaR and CVaR use a 95% parametric normal approximation and are displayed as estimated annual losses.

03

Macro shocks

Scenario returns are transparent, hypothetical shocks rather than claims about future market performance.

04

Projection engine

Monte Carlo paths use seeded random draws, monthly contributions, and the portfolio's annualized inputs.

Educational tool only. The assumptions are illustrative, are not live market forecasts, and do not constitute investment advice.
